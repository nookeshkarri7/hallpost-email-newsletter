Hallpost: Email Newsletter

Hallpost is a small app for the volunteers who send a church, school, club or charity newsletter. It sends from the group's own Gmail, Zoho, Yahoo or iCloud address, one email at a time, under the mailbox's daily limit, and keeps the member list on your own computer or phone.

Get it from Microsoft Get it on Google Play

Why it exists

Mailchimp's free plan now covers 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, so a parish or PTA with a few hundred families has to pay every month. Hallpost is $39 once on Windows (free for up to 100 contacts) and free with ads on Android.

Features

  • Member import from CSV (Google Contacts, Excel, Outlook), with a reason for every skipped row
  • Four templates with a live preview, {first_name} personalisation and a test send
  • Paced sending under your daily limit, resuming tomorrow from the same member
  • Reply-STOP unsubscribe list honoured on every list
  • SPF and DMARC check for your own domain
  • No tracking pixels, no analytics, no account
